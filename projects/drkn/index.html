01 / THE CHALLENGE Market information is abundant; a usable sequence of decisions is harder to find. A price chart can sit beside a headline, a derivatives metric and an order-book snapshot without explaining how they relate. More panels do not automatically produce a clearer picture. The starting point for DRKN was to reduce that fragmentation while keeping the underlying evidence available.
02 / DESIGN SYSTEM The visual system combines obsidian surfaces, soft mint and a warm copper accent. Mint gives the product its recognizable identity; copper introduces contrast in selected labels, navigation states and editorial details. The palette creates hierarchy without turning every component into an alert. Sculptural glass and metal imagery gives the brand an expressive layer beyond conventional financial dashboards.
03 / PRODUCT EXPERIENCE The chat workspace is the product's primary entry into research. Instead of asking a new user to choose from a wall of tools, it offers a readable prompt, a compact composer and a few relevant suggestions. Navigation separates conversation and markets from supporting account and connection tasks. More detailed actions remain available without occupying the center of the experience.
04 / MOBILE INTERACTION The mobile experience needed its own layout decisions. A narrow screen changes the relationship between navigation, suggestions, the conversation and the keyboard. DRKN uses a compact header, a single hamburger entry and a composer that responds to the available viewport. Suggested starting points remain close to the input rather than becoming a separate dashboard to navigate.
05 / RESEARCH PIPELINE The evidence-led web research path combines validated closed candles, technical indicators, higher-timeframe context and optional derivatives information. News and executed order flow add context where coverage is available. These inputs have different time horizons and meanings: a short flow window cannot establish a daily trend, and displayed order-book liquidity is not the same thing as completed trades.
06 / MARKET PATTERNS Market-pattern discovery runs independently of a visitor opening the page. A dedicated worker maintains prepared results across a selected liquid-market universe and several timeframes. The frontend reads those results, so changing a filter or returning to the workspace does not trigger a new round of heavy candle retrieval and geometric analysis.
07 / CONNECTED EXPERIENCE The Telegram assistant extends DRKN into a place users already visit. A concise welcome, a coherent visual identity and contextual actions help connect the bot to the broader product. The aim is continuity: someone can discover a tool in Telegram and continue into the web workspace without feeling that they have entered an unrelated application.
08 / ACCOUNT & ENGAGEMENT The account area brings identity, credits, exchange connections and session information together without repeating the same settings in several menus. A compact profile summary introduces the person and their available credits. Security starts with the current device; deeper session information is disclosed when requested. The interface should communicate what can be managed, not overwhelm the user with decorative statistics.
09 / ENGINEERING & VALIDATION DRKN's architecture separates the HTTP application, market-data services, research logic and background scanning. That separation lets the interface read prepared information while workers continue their own schedules. Publication, cache behavior and recovery matter as much as the first screenshot: a refined visual design is of little use if an old browser tab loads incompatible assets or a phone sees unstyled content before initialization finishes.
10 / THE DIRECTION The work on DRKN connects brand expression, a focused conversational interface and the underlying behavior of a research system. The sculptural imagery establishes character; the restrained workspace creates room to think. Mobile and Telegram extend that language into different contexts, while shared account flows help those contexts feel connected.

DRKN

DRKN is a crypto research product built around a simple question: how can a trader understand a complex market without having to navigate an equally complex interface? It brings conversational research, chart patterns, market context, account connections and a Telegram assistant into one connected experience.

My role spans product direction, visual identity, interaction design, frontend development and backend integration. The challenge was to connect the expressive qualities of a crafted digital brand with the practical discipline of a research tool: readable information, explicit assumptions and consistent behavior across desktop, mobile and Telegram.

The result is an evolving product rather than a static concept. The interface is live, background workers prepare market-pattern results, and research outputs distinguish observed evidence from conditional scenarios. This case study follows the thinking behind that system, the visual language that holds it together, and the engineering choices that support it.
